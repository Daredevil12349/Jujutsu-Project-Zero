## 1. Identity (G12 official listing text; G13 fan wiki)
- Listed as `[SANDBOX]Jujutsu: RETRO` by **CAVE studio**; described as a "mostly COOP retro-styled Jujutsu Kaisen fighting game". (G12)
- Roblox record dates per the fan wiki: created **2026-10-09**, last updated **2026-11-16** (the wiki notes these are record dates, not launch dates). Max players listed as 25. (G13)
- The fan wiki states it is independent and not affiliated with CAVE studio. Treat as REPORTED.

## 2. Modes (G12)
| Mode | Description from the listing |
|---|---|
| **Initiation** | Survive waves of cursed spirits (solo or with friends), awaken random Cursed Techniques, grow stronger after every wave; choose upgrades, unlock rare abilities, obtain Cursed Tools, face bosses |
| **Culling Game** | Enter one of three colonies; hunt curses, eliminate rival sorcerers, earn points, discover abilities in the world; "only the strongest will remain" |
Other listed features: unique Cursed Techniques and Domain Expansions, destructible environments, freestyle combat, rare traits/upgrades/Cursed Tools, PvE survival and PvP.

## 3. Reported mechanics (fan wiki, G13; each could be outdated)
- Technique kit example (Mahito's Idle Transfiguration): moves on Z, X, C, F+C (clones), F+Z (form switch); V is locked until a Black Flash is achieved. (REPORTED)
- Domain Expansion example costs **1200 CE** in the technique panel; the activation key was reported inconsistently (F per prompt, T in a demo). (REPORTED, discrepancy noted by the wiki)
- A top-right **Sandbox / PvP switcher** toggles between a flat test map ("safe") and PvP. (REPORTED)
- Between waves the player picks from **blessing cards** (run-long upgrades): e.g. Max Cursed Energy +17% (Rare), Cursed Power +5% (Uncommon), Health +10% (Rare); a Domain Expansion can appear as a Legendary card. (REPORTED, observed in a recording per the wiki)
- HUD elements reported: level-up popups, exorcism streak counters, currency, grade progress from Grade 4 toward Grade 2. (REPORTED)
