## 1. Identity
- Roblox listing text: "a Jujutsu Kaisen inspired fighting game set 1000 years after the original story" (G17, OFFICIAL-LISTING). The listing also references code releases tied to like counts (G17).

## 2. Reported systems (G18, REPORTED; tier lists are opinion)
- **Races** with rarity: Human (Common), Cursed Spirit (Common), Mutated Corpse (Legendary), among others. One race reportedly grants a free Cursed Technique skill before completing "Yuki's Trial". Races affect combat, survivability, damage bonuses and progression.
- **Clans** with rarity and passive bonuses, e.g. Okkotsu (Mythic; automatically unlocks the Rika cursed technique when you obtain your technique), Itadori and Hakari (reported +75 HP, chance of infinite CE and health regeneration), Sugawara (Rare; permanent +5 HP).
- **Cursed Techniques** ranked S to D (3 in S tier at time of writing); a reinforcement-style technique reportedly absorbs hits and reduces damage, with Blood Throw as a close-range attack.
- Clan/race/technique are three separate layers of identity.
