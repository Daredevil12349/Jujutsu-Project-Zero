## Rules
1. No assets (models, animations, audio, textures, maps) or scripts copied from other games.
2. IDs are recorded only after the asset is verified in Studio/Creator Store. **No placeholder or guessed IDs** (`UNVERIFIED` entries stay blank).
3. Creator Store assets: record creator, license/terms as shown, and date checked.
4. Third-party IP (Jujutsu Kaisen names, characters): treat as inspiration; the project owner is responsible for evaluating Roblox policy and rights-holder risk before any public release. `UNVERIFIED`: no legal review done.
5. Original assets: record author and creation date.
